# Eden roadmap: inner life, mapped before building

Status: **step 1 built (0.3.0, 15 November 2026); steps 2–5 not built.** A separate prototype, 0.4.0-proto, tests the moving bar behind a flag (`docs/PROTO-MOVING-BAR.md`). It is not step 2 and it does not turn on in the app. Robert's choices: Eden only; learned trust including the Creator's word (step 4); self-model measures in step 2.
Rule for every step: the *rules* are written in, the *outcomes* are not. Every step must:
- keep the drift check green;
- add a THEORY-CHECK row;
- come with a test showing the result could have gone either way.

## Step 1 — Senses and needs ("maintain senses and life")
- **What they get:**
  - Sight range depends on condition. Starving, parched or exhausted creatures see less; it recovers with food, water and rest.
  - Two new needs: **water** (springs to find and drink from) and **health** (falls on rough ground, illness after deprivation, slow recovery when rested).
  - Warmth stays as exposure after release (clothing).
- **Built in:** body needs, how fast they rise, what restores them.
- **Left to emerge:** how each creature balances them, and where it goes.
- **Figures:** water is lost about seven times faster than food. People survive roughly 3 days without water and roughly 3 weeks without food (a common rule of thumb), so thirst rises about 7× faster than hunger. Sight shrinks with fatigue, as in real vigilance studies (to be cited when built).
- **Creator's guidance:** before release it also covers thirst and injury. Before release no one dies of thirst either.
- **Test it's not rigged:** two worlds identical except one creature's condition. The fitter creature sees more but isn't guaranteed to find more; it depends on where food is.
- **You can then test:** does guidance keep creatures aware and whole, and what happens to awareness after release?

## Step 2 — Learned wants and fears
- **What they get:**
  - **Wants:** places, sources and actions that relieved a need gain pull.
  - **Fears:** places and actions tied to harm (starving, injury, a death witnessed nearby) gain aversion that fades unless renewed.
  - Their state changes how they weigh things: tired or starving means cautious, but desperation can push towards risk.
- **Built in:** one learning rule for everyone (the same reinforcement as today's self-judgement), plus inherited temperament (curiosity, and a new trait, caution, inherited the same way).
- **Left to emerge:** what each creature ends up wanting or fearing.
- **Test it's not rigged:** two creatures with identical temperament but different histories must end with different wants and fears, and identical histories with identical ones.
- **Theory link:** before release, do the Creator's overrides prevent fears from forming, because they never suffer the harm? Measurable, not assumed.

## Step 3 — Bonds and witnessing others
- **What they get:**
  - **Bonds** form with kin and with creatures they spend time near or share food with.
  - **Witnessing:** seeing a bonded creature suffer or thrive shapes the witness's own wants and fears.
  - **Sharing:** a fed creature may share with a hungry bonded one, at a cost to itself. Today only parents feed young children.
- **Built in:** how bonds grow and fade, and the cost of sharing.
- **Left to emerge:** who shares with whom, and whether groups form.
- **Test it's not rigged:** sharing must be able to lower the sharer's survival. Nothing rewards being generous.

## Step 4 — Communication and trust
- **What they get:** a signal ("food here", "danger here") a creature may send when it finds something.
  - Listeners keep a **trust** score per speaker, learned only from whether that speaker's past signals turned out true.
  - Eden link: the Creator's warning and the serpent's claim become two speakers whose words are weighed by trust. Credence then comes from experience instead of a fixed formula.
- **Built in:** the channel exists, plus the trust-update rule.
- **Left to emerge:** whether signals become honest, ignored or exploited.
- **Note:** this was deferred in the original spec and in Robert's first review instructions. Robert has now asked for it. Logged as a scope change.
- **Test it's not rigged:** an honest speaker and a lying speaker start with equal trust. Trust must separate through experience alone.

## Step 5 — Materials and know-how
- **What they get:**
  - **Materials** to find on the island: wood, stone, hides, seed.
  - **Recipes** a creature knows only after discovering them (trial at a cost) or being **taught** through step 4.
  - **Production** needs both the know-how and the materials: field, clothing, tool, shelter.
  - Before release they need none of it. After release, the garden's ease is gone.
- **Built in:** recipes, material costs and work time, at realistic early-farming and craft scales.
- **Left to emerge:** who discovers what, how know-how spreads, and whether it builds up across generations.
- **Test it's not rigged:** discovery chance and teaching success are written in, but which creature discovers what, and whether a population ever gets tools, varies by seed.
